I break into lab machines and write down every step, including the ones that go
nowhere. A post starts with nothing but an IP or a running app, and ends at root.

These are written for people who are learning. A term gets explained the first
time it shows up instead of assuming you already know it, and every post carries
a "Run it yourself" section so you can rebuild the target and follow along on
your own machine.

<p class="intro__note">Everything here runs against my own lab or a public training target. Never point these techniques at a system you do not own or have permission to test.</p>
