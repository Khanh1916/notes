I break into lab machines and write down every step, dead ends included. Each
post starts from an IP or a running app and ends at root.

Written for people who are learning: terms are explained the first time they
appear, and every post has a "Run it yourself" section so you can rebuild the
target and follow along.

<p class="intro__note">Everything here runs against my own lab or a public training target. Never point these techniques at a system you do not own or have permission to test.</p>
